1. Executive Summary

MetroOps is an enterprise-grade wholesale & distribution operations management system developed to replace error-prone spreadsheets with an automated, transactional platform. It provides seamless synchronization across wholesale customer relationships, real-time SKU inventory tracking, atomic order dispatch challans, and GST-compliant tax invoicing.

2. System Architecture & Tech Stack

  • Frontend SPA: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Vanilla CSS3 Design System with responsive grid layouts.
  • Backend REST API: Node.js, Express, TypeScript, modular controller-service pattern.
  • Database & ORM: PostgreSQL 15 on cloud, Prisma ORM with native ACID transactions.
  • Validation & Security: Zod runtime schema validation, JWT Bearer tokens, BCrypt password hashing, Helmet, CORS protection.

4. Core Business Logic & Data Integrity

A. Atomic Stock Deductions (Zero Negative Stock)

When a sales challan is confirmed, all line items are validated against available warehouse stock within an atomic database transaction. If any SKU lacks sufficient units, the entire operation is rolled back with an informative error message.

B. Snapshot Immutability

Historical orders are decoupled from ongoing catalog edits. When line items are added to a challan, snapshots of the product name, SKU, and unit price are permanently stored in the database.

C. Goods & Services Tax (GSTIN) Compliance

Every sales challan generates an itemized, printable delivery challan and tax invoice adhering to GST regulations with company and customer GST numbers.

6. Known Limitations & Roadmap

  1. Multi-Warehouse Support: Tracking inventory across geographically distributed warehouses with transfer challans.
  2. Government E-Way Bill Integration: Automatic direct filing for consignments exceeding statutory thresholds.
  3. Barcode Scanner Support: Mobile camera scanning for high-volume picking and packing.
